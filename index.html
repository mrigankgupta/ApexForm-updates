Your privacy

The camera feed never leaves your Mac.

Posture detection runs on Apple's Vision framework, on your machine, in memory. We couldn't watch you if we wanted to — there's no server to send anything to.

On-device Vision

Frames are analyzed by Apple's Vision framework on your Mac and immediately discarded.

Works offline
