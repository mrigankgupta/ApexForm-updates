Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This policy describes how ApexForm (the macOS menu-bar posture coach) handles your information.

Summary

ApexForm processes your camera and optional AirPods motion entirely on your Mac. We do not collect, store, or transmit video, audio recordings, or body images to our servers — because we do not operate servers for this app.

Information processed on your device

When you grant permission, the app may use:

  • Camera — to detect face and body pose for posture coaching and movement-break counting.
  • AirPods motion (optional) — to improve head-posture detection when worn.

These inputs are analyzed in memory in real time. No video or motion recordings are saved to disk or sent over the internet.

Information stored on your device

The app stores small amounts of data locally on your Mac:

DataPurpose
Settings (sensitivity, schedule, alerts)Your preferences
Calibration baselines (angles and ratios)Compare your current posture to your “good” posture
Daily activity counters (e.g. squats, blinks)Menu-bar stats and motivation

These values contain no photos, video, or personally identifying information beyond what your Mac already knows about you as the logged-in user.

What we do not do

  • No account sign-up required for core features
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs in the current release
  • No sale of personal data
  • No cloud upload of camera or sensor data

Network access

Posture coaching and movement detection work offline. The Mac app may use the network only to check for updates (Sparkle) against a public feed, and later to open a Pro checkout page in your browser when purchase is live. Camera and motion data are never uploaded.

Permissions

macOS will ask you to allow Camera and Motion & Fitness (AirPods) access. You can revoke these at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Without camera access, posture features that depend on vision will not work; AirPods-only mode remains available when motion access is granted.

Apple Health (Pro, opt-in)

If you enable Apple Health sync, ApexForm writes standing sessions and squat/twist movement breaks to Health as workouts. ApexForm never reads data from Health. You can turn this off at any time in Settings or revoke access under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when features change (for example, adding optional analytics or Mac App Store distribution). The “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
